## Verified technical controls

- Canonicals and indexing metadata only appear when `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` is set. Domainless local builds remain `noindex, nofollow` and omit canonicals.
- The 4 HOLD topics are absent from the output, navigation, sitemap, and internal links.
- The social image is a real 1920×1080 official Steam promotional JPEG. Its 16:9 frame may be center-cropped by some social platforms but has sufficient resolution.
- PNG icons are real 512×512, 32×32, and 180×180 images. The ICO contains 16, 32, 48, 64, 128, and 256 px entries.
- Lighthouse passed color contrast, heading order, descriptive link text, responsive image sizing, and browser-console checks.
