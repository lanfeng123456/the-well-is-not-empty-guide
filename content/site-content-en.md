## 2. `/walkthrough/` — complete route

**SEO title:** THE WELL IS NOT EMPTY Walkthrough: Full Route  
**Meta description:** Complete the released game with a concise route through the village, prison, Storage and Surface facility, capacitor puzzle, Nursery and finale.  
**H1:** THE WELL IS NOT EMPTY Full Walkthrough

> **Spoiler level:** Full progression spoilers. The ending details are kept on a separate page.

### 1. Finish the village first

Search the houses and speak to every available neighbor before using the Well entrance. The official **Social Network** description makes this a hard cutoff: conversations must happen before you enter. Pick up the highlighted opening item, finish the dialogue loop, then descend.

### 2. Learn the prison survival loop

Take the Rusty Spoon and use it to break marked rubble and remove rats blocking your route. Stay back from prisoner bars until you know where you are going; grabs trigger changing on-screen QTE keys. Locate the hand-crank generator before entering a dark danger room. Clear the approach first, crank the machine second, then run while the light remains active.

Collect every obvious lever handle or key on the main path and return to the matching mechanism. Use the **Prison Main** sign as your route anchor. On beams, make small corrections toward the center instead of holding one direction.

### 3. Navigate the facility by signs

Read the facility map and use **Storage**, **Surface**, **Block A**, **Tools Storage**, **Radio Room**, **Freezer** and **Dumping Site Gate Control** as landmarks. Search currently open rooms before testing every locked door again. Complete the Dr. Cobo sequence, then continue until you recover the **Uncharged Capacitor** and reach the Battery Charge Station.

### 4. Reset the charge station

Pull the main lever, wait for the generated signal, use the nearby visual clue to send the matching digit, and repeat until all seven signals are accepted. A wrong digit restarts the equipment. After the reset, place the Uncharged Capacitor in the charger, wait for the ready state, retrieve it and use it at the downstream power panel.

### 5. Complete the Nursery route

Use the Nursery Key, inspect Julian and finish the missing-object objective instead of forcing the next passage. Follow Merkert’s sequence and the hand/light symbols. When the lantern becomes available, use `F` to toggle it. If a fixture says a piece is missing, search the connected side path and bring the highlighted object back.

### 6. Reach the finale

Follow the lantern and symbol route into the ritual area. Complete the live prompts during the final creature/escape sequence, then continue through the viewpoint montage and closing return. For the story interpretation and hidden-achievement caveat, continue to the ending page.

## 3. `/village-before-the-well/`

**SEO title:** What to Do Before Entering the Well  
**Meta description:** Avoid the opening missable in THE WELL IS NOT EMPTY with a spoiler-light village checklist for the Social Network achievement.  
**H1:** Village Checklist Before Entering the Well

Do not enter the Well as soon as the route opens if you want **Social Network**. Steam defines the achievement as “Speak to all your neighbors before entering The Well,” making the village the game’s clearest early missable checkpoint.

### Safe opening checklist

1. Walk the full accessible village loop.
2. Approach every neighbor or occupied interaction point and exhaust the available conversation.
3. Recheck paths around the houses before committing to the Well.
4. Pick up the highlighted opening progression item.
5. Enter only after you are satisfied every conversation has been completed.

The release runs show several named conversations, but this guide does not yet have an independently reproduced, complete NPC-name list. The safe method is therefore spatial: clear the whole accessible village rather than relying on a short list that might omit a neighbor.

### Can I come back after entering?

The achievement wording treats entering the Well as the cutoff. Plan as though the opening conversations are no longer recoverable in that run. If the achievement does not unlock, retry the short opening and search every accessible house/path before descending.

### No-death run tip

If you also want **Untouchable**, completing Social Network at the start of the same run is efficient. The village is low risk compared with the underground route, and it prevents a second achievement cleanup run later.

## 4. `/prison-walkthrough/`

**SEO title:** Prison Walkthrough: Spoon, Rubble & Power  
**Meta description:** Clear Prison Main with the Rusty Spoon, timed hand-crank generators, prisoner QTEs and safer balance-beam tactics.  
**H1:** Prison Walkthrough

The early prison teaches nearly every survival rule used later. Progress is less about combat than preparing a route before the environment turns dangerous.

### Rusty Spoon and rubble

Pick up the Rusty Spoon when the route presents it. This unlocks **Your only friend...** and gives you the tool used against breakable rubble and underground pests. Search behind cleared debris; the critical path and optional objects are easy to miss in the same dark corners.

### Hand-crank generators

Generators buy a limited window of light. Before cranking:

1. Locate the doorway or gate you need to reach.
2. Clear any rubble and learn the turns between the generator and exit.
3. Return to the crank and activate it.
4. Move immediately through the danger room.

If the light fails, retreat to a known safe point and reset the attempt. Repeatedly cranking before learning the path wastes time and makes the route feel random.

### Prisoner grabs and QTEs

Keep space between yourself and barred prisoners. When a grab begins, watch the current on-screen key: the prompt changes, so a memorized single button is unreliable. Complete the sequence cleanly and move away from the bars before reorienting.

### Beams and Prison Main

Use **Prison Main** as the signed destination. On a balance beam, tap toward center rather than holding a correction. Achievement hunters can remain on a stable portion for 60 seconds to attempt **Well Balanced**; completion-focused players should cross without stopping.

Search every currently open room in one branch before retrying a locked door. Read nearby notes and inspect highlighted shelves, tables and containers. Complete the interaction and continue searching the adjoining rooms; do not treat the area as a dead-end lore scene. Highlighted art and small objects may be missable, but a verified 100% collectible count is not available yet.

### Freezer and gate-control route

Use the room signs to keep the Freezer/medical side distinct from the gate-control objective. If a door only repeats that a key is required, leave it marked in your mental map and clear another open branch. The critical power chain eventually supplies the **Uncharged Capacitor** and sends you to the Battery Charge Station.

### Before leaving the facility

Confirm that you have reset the charge station, charged the capacitor and installed the resulting power item where the route requires it. The newly powered/opened gate leads toward the red late-game tunnels.

## 6. `/battery-charge-station-puzzle/`

**SEO title:** Battery Charge Station Puzzle Solution  
**Meta description:** Reset the random signal generator, send all seven correct digits, charge the Uncharged Capacitor and reopen the power route.  
**H1:** Battery Charge Station Puzzle Solution

There is no safe universal seven-digit code for the Battery Charge Station. The room generates a signal, and the nearby clue tells you which digit matches that signal. Solve one result at a time.

### Verified reset procedure

1. Bring the **Uncharged Capacitor** to the charge-station room.
2. Pull the main lever to start the random signal generator.
3. Wait until the current signal is visible.
4. Compare it with the nearby visual clue.
5. Send the matching digit using the station controls.
6. Repeat for all seven generated signals.

The instruction sheet warns that one incorrect digit restarts the equipment. Do not rush the controls or attempt to memorize a number from somebody else’s run. Keep the clue in view, translate the current signal, then enter only that digit.

### Charging the capacitor

Once the seven-step reset is accepted, take the Uncharged Capacitor to the wall charger. Use the equipped item when prompted, wait for the machine’s ready/charged state, then retrieve the power item. Carry it to the downstream panel controlling the blocked route. When the panel accepts it, the gate/path ahead becomes available.

### If the puzzle keeps resetting

- Confirm you waited for a new signal before pressing a digit.
- Read the clue in the same orientation shown at the station.
- Enter one digit per generated state.
- If a wrong input resets the machine, restart from the lever and rebuild the seven-step sequence.

**SEO title:** Controls: Interact, Items, Lantern & QTEs  
**Meta description:** Verified PC controls for interaction, held items, the lantern, prisoner QTEs and balance sections, plus the current controller-map evidence limit.  
**H1:** THE WELL IS NOT EMPTY Controls

The release build teaches controls as they become relevant. The verified keyboard prompts are:

| Action | Input |
|---|---|
| Interact / pick up | `E` |
| Toggle lantern | `F` |
| Cycle held items | `R` (developer-confirmed for the earlier level and consistent with launch behavior) |
| Escape grabs / scripted actions | Press the changing on-screen key |
| Balance | Correct left/right toward the center |

The capacitor sequence also shows a mouse-button icon for using the equipped item, but the captured prompt does not label left versus right in text. Follow the icon shown by your build instead of relying on an unverified binding table.

### QTE and balance tips

Prisoner grabs use changing keys. Watch the current prompt and release old inputs between steps. On beams, use short corrections; holding one direction creates a larger swing that is harder to recover.

### Controller support

Steam lists full controller support and DualShock support. The launch evidence reviewed for this guide does not show a complete controller binding screen, so exact gamepad buttons are intentionally omitted until they are reproduced in-game.

## 10. `/achievements/`

**SEO title:** All 8 THE WELL IS NOT EMPTY Achievements  
**Meta description:** Official requirements and evidence-safe strategies for all eight Steam achievements, including Social Network, Well Balanced, Untouchable and hidden A New Age.  
**H1:** All 8 Steam Achievements

THE WELL IS NOT EMPTY has eight Steam achievements.

### Your only friend...

**Requirement:** Pick up the Rusty Spoon. Take it on the early underground route; it is the main tool for rubble and pests.

### Social Network

**Requirement:** Speak to all your neighbors before entering The Well. Clear every accessible village conversation before descending. Treat entry as the missable cutoff.

### You Monster

**Requirement:** Kill a Shadow Lurker. The objective is official, but a repeatable kill setup was not isolated in the checked runs. Do not risk a no-death attempt while experimenting.

### A New Age

**Requirement:** Hidden. The internal Steam key `BEAT_GAME_ACH` suggests main-story completion, but this remains an evidence-labeled inference.

### Sing us a song!

**Requirement:** Listen to The Piano Man’s song. The internal key `ALLSHEETS_ACH` links it to sheet music. One player reports three pieces, including one in a cell and one behind the Foreman’s Key door; the third location is still unverified.

### Heart Surgeon

**Requirement:** Give Julian his heart back. Complete the Nursery objective chain and perform the return interaction when presented.

### Well Balanced

**Requirement:** Balance on a beam for one minute. Choose a stable section, make small corrections and remain on the beam for 60 seconds.

### Untouchable

**Requirement:** Complete the game without dying a single time. Use a fresh run, pre-plan every generator route, avoid unnecessary collectible detours, obey QTE prompts and cross beams conservatively.
